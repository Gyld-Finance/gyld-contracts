## What Went Well
- **Layout-safety proved, not assumed.** The storage append was validated three ways: append-only to the ERC-7201 struct, the slot-pinning test extended to pins offsets 3/4 (`test_storageLayout_documentFieldsAppendedAtOffsets3and4`), and a `test_upgrade_preservesDocuments` test proving state survives `upgradeToAndCall`. This is exactly the right pattern for UUPS storage changes and gave review immediate confidence.
- **Research caught the factory question early.** The `bondTokenLogic` immutable bind (baked into proxy initcode) was identified in research and confirmed by the implementer, so "no factory can deploy new series on the new implementation" surfaced as a documented finding, not a review surprise.
- **Scope discipline.** Gen-2 Sepolia/BSC proxies (do-not-upgrade) untouched; `TokenFactory` correctly left out of scope; off-chain Arweave/S3 + PDF-hash pipeline explicitly carved out as a separate ticket.
- **Deviations were flagged, so review was frictionless.** Swap-and-pop removal, true upsert (no array bloat on replace), and the decision to leave `DOCUMENT_ROLE` ungranted at initialize (relying on `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` as role admin) were all called out in the implementation comment — the one deviation from the plan was easy to adjudicate at review.

## What Could Be Improved
- **Plan-vs-delivery gap on rollout-stage deliverables.** The plan's success criteria included the invariant-handler extension (Phase 2) and the `UpgradeBondTokenERC1643.s.sol` upgrade script + `DEPLOYMENTS.md` rollout entries (Phase 3). Neither was delivered; the reviewer had to re-list them as "non-blocking, track in the rollout ticket." Because rollout/deploy is a later stage, there is real risk these scoped artifacts either vanish or are rediscovered only at deploy time, when they are the critical path (timelock-scheduled upgrade over live proxies).

## Learnings
1. **Rollout-stage artifacts scoped in a plan don't get delivered by the implementer — hand them to the deploy/rollout ticket explicitly.** When a plan lists an upgrade/deploy script, deployment-registry (`DEPLOYMENTS.md`) rollout entries, or an invariant-handler extension, either deliver them in the implementation or pass them as a named, tracked item to the rollout ticket — otherwise they silently drop out of the pipeline and reappear on the deploy critical path.
2. **Verify the factory's implementation bind at research, not at deploy.** An immutable-logic factory (address pinned at construction and baked into proxy initcode) means a UUPS implementation upgrade only lifts *existing* proxies — new series still need a new factory or a logic-address setter. Confirm the bind mechanism up front so it's a documented finding, and fold a factory follow-up into the rollout ticket.
3. **Append-only storage + slot-pin + upgrade-preservation test is the proof of UUPS layout safety.** Extending an ERC-7201 struct is safe only if fields are appended (never reordered) and the proof is a raw-storage slot-pin for the new offsets plus an upgrade-preservation test — a stated-intention comment is not proof. Preserve this pattern for every future upgradeable-contract change.
4. **Flagging deviations at handoff keeps a single-pass review clean.** Every intentional deviation (swap-and-pop, true upsert, role-grant timing) was declared in the implementation comment, so the reviewer could formally approve rather than re-litigate. Keep "enumerate every deviation at handoff" as the standing norm.
5. **Carve out out-of-scope companion work as a named ticket.** The off-chain upload / PDF-hash pipeline and the gen-2 do-not-upgrade proxies were clearly bounded, preventing scope creep; mirror this by naming the companion ticket in the plan and rollout handoff.

## Process Improvement Suggestions
- **Plan template: separate "contract + tests" deliverables from "deploy/rollout" deliverables**, and require an explicit handoff line for anything deferred (e.g. "Phase N deliverables deferred to rollout ticket X"). The reviewer shouldn't have to re-enumerate them.
- **Prologue the rollout/deploy stage with the plan's deferred checker**: when a feature is onchain-deployed, the rollout ticket should open with a checklist of plan-named upgrade-script / DEPLOYMENTS / invariant items to confirm none were dropped.
- Consider making `DEPLOYMENTS.md` rollout entries and the upgrade script part of the implementation's *definition of done* for onchain features, since they are the actual production delivery path.
